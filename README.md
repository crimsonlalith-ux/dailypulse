# DailyPulse ⚡ - Global Trends, Fluency & Social Radar

A sleek, modern progressive web application designed to help you stay ahead of internet culture, improve your English communication, and track trending topics across YouTube, Reddit, X (Twitter), and World News.

---

## 🚀 How to Run It on Your Laptop

### Option 1: Direct Double-Click (Fastest)
1. Go to folder: `C:\Users\Lalith sanjay\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\dailypulse`
2. Double-click `index.html`. It will instantly open in your default browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, etc.)!

### Option 2: Run a Local Server (Enables PWA Install)
Open PowerShell in this folder and run:
```powershell
python -m http.server 8080
```
Then open: [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080)

---

## 📱 How to Use It on Your Phone or Tablet

1. Ensure your laptop and phone are on the same Wi-Fi.
2. Run `python -m http.server 8080` on your laptop.
3. Check your laptop's local IP address (run `ipconfig` in PowerShell, e.g., `192.168.1.5`).
4. On your phone/tablet browser (Chrome or Safari), visit:
   `http://YOUR_LAPTOP_IP:8080` (e.g., `http://192.168.1.5:8080`)
5. Tap **Share / Settings Menu (`⋮`)** and choose:
   **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**.
6. DailyPulse will now look and behave like a full-screen native mobile app with its own icon on your phone!

## 🎴 Features Included:
1. **The 5-Card Daily Deck:** Slang vs Formal, 60-Sec Trend Byte, Quick Reply Challenge, Micro-Study, and Icon Spotlight.
2. **Audio Pronunciation:** Tap 🔊 on any phrase to hear natural English pronunciation.
3. **4-in-1 Social Media Radar:** Live filters for YouTube (Fireship, Lessons in Meme Culture, MKBHD), Reddit (r/ProgrammerHumor, r/me_irl), Twitter/X, and World News.
4. **Pulse AI Trend Mentor:** Tap `[🤖 Ask AI]` on any radar item to decode the meme, get sentence suggestions, or chat freely.
5. **Anti-Burnout Streak Tracker:** Tracks your daily streak in your browser memory so you build a real habit without quitting after 2 days.
